# Settings

The company's settings: its logo, who owns it and how buyers reach it. I designed these screens in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here they are rebuilt from the design system's components, and they work. This page is in progress: the Account tab is built; API key, Payment settings and Exchange rate come next.

**Try it:** press the logo, upload any picture, crop it and **Save**; press it again to **Edit logo** or **Remove** (and **Undo**). **Change owner**: choose a member and confirm. **Edit contact**, or empty it and add a new one.

## What the page has to get right

- **The logo buyers see.** It shows on the payment checkout, so it is cropped round right here, and can be cropped again from the original.
- **Ownership changes on purpose.** Only the owner hands it on, to a confirmed member, and confirms first: the previous owner becomes an Admin and can't take it back.
- **A way for buyers to get help.** A link or any contact, up to 200 characters, shown on the payment receipt.

## Decided while building it

- **The logo is the button.** Figma made the pencil badge open the menu; here the whole logo does, and the badge stays a picture (the Avatar rule). Without a logo it opens *Upload logo*, with one a menu: *Edit logo*, and *Remove* in red.
- **Remove at once, with Undo.** No question first: a toast says *Logo removed* and offers *Undo*.
- **The crop is Image Crop** from [File Upload](?path=/docs/components-file-upload--docs), darkened around the circle with the overlay of modals and drawers, not Figma's white veil.
- **Change the owner, then confirm.** Only confirmed members are listed (not you, not someone still invited), nothing is chosen at first, and *Save changes* waits for a choice. The list is a Radio group with its title, *Members*, and a search above it. A question follows in the same modal: *Make … the owner?*. Then you are an Admin, the button is gone, and Members shows the new owner.
- **One text for the contact**, in Add and Edit: *Add a support link or any contact info. Buyers see it on the payment receipt.* Figma had two, one with a slip. *Add contact* has a plus; an empty contact asks for something; emptying it in Edit removes it.
- **Errors as an example.** *Something went wrong, please try again* is shown in *When saving fails*, not in the live flow.
- **All four tabs, three not available yet**, until their screens are built.
- **Test data**: our test logo, the owner from Members, an invented support link.
