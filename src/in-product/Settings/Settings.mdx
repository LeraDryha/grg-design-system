# Settings

The company's settings: its logo, who owns it and how buyers reach it. I designed these screens in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here they are rebuilt from the design system's components, and they work. This page is in progress: the Account, API key and Payment settings tabs are built; Exchange rate comes next.

**Try it:** press the logo, upload any picture, crop it and **Save**; press it again to **Edit logo** or **Remove** (and **Undo**). **Change owner**: choose a member and confirm. **Edit contact**, or empty it and add a new one. On **API key**: create a key (a name, permissions, an IP white list), copy it or scan its QR code, open it in the list, edit or delete it, or **Delete all**. On **Payment settings**: turn fixed sum invoice or auto-convert on with a currency, and off.

## What the page has to get right

- **The logo buyers see.** It shows on the payment checkout, so it is cropped round right here, and can be cropped again from the original.
- **Ownership changes on purpose.** Only the owner hands it on, to a confirmed member, and confirms first: the previous owner becomes an Admin and can't take it back.
- **A way for buyers to get help.** A link or any contact, up to 200 characters, shown on the payment receipt.
- **Payments that settle as the business wants.** Fixed sum invoice pays out the exact fiat amount; auto-convert turns crypto into one currency.
- **Keys with only the access they need.** Each API key has its permissions and, if wanted, the IP addresses it works from; the secret is shown once, to copy or scan.

## Decided while building it

- **The logo is the button.** Figma made the pencil badge open the menu; here the whole logo does, and the badge stays a picture (the Avatar rule). Without a logo it opens *Upload logo*, with one a menu: *Edit logo*, and *Remove* in red.
- **Remove at once, with Undo.** No question first: a toast says *Logo removed* and offers *Undo*.
- **The crop is Image Crop** from [File Upload](?path=/docs/components-file-upload--docs), darkened around the circle with the overlay of modals and drawers, not Figma's white veil.
- **Change the owner, then confirm.** Only confirmed members are listed (not you, not someone still invited), nothing is chosen at first, and *Save changes* waits for a choice. The list is a Radio group with its title, *Members*, and a search above it. A question follows in the same modal: *Make … the owner?*. Then you are an Admin, the button is gone, and Members shows the new owner.
- **One text for the contact**, in Add and Edit: *Add a support link or any contact info. Buyers see it on the payment receipt.* Figma had two, one with a slip. *Add contact* has a plus; an empty contact asks for something; emptying it in Edit removes it.
- **Errors as an example.** *Something went wrong, please try again* is shown in *When saving fails*, not in the live flow.
- **All four tabs; Exchange rate not available yet**, until its screens are built.

### API key

- **The IP white list is the Multi-value Input** (Figma's second version): type an address, press Enter. Only IPv4 or IPv6 addresses are added; a wrong one (Figma's example has 224.876.103.42) says so under the field.
- **The keys in read-only fields with Copy**, the Figma version that matches [Input](?path=/docs/components-input--docs) (*Copy a referral link*), and a real QR code of the private key, black on white in both themes. The result stays in the modal (not a toast): the keys must be saved. *Shown only once: save it now* under the private key.
- **A key opens under its row** ([Table](?path=/docs/components-table--docs) `expansion`): its permissions and IP addresses. The chevron sits before the actions, as the Table's other chevrons do.
- **Edit is the same modal** with the name, permissions and addresses; the keys themselves don't change.
- **Create waits** for a name and at least one permission.
- **Texts without the brand**: the Withdrawal description drops the product name and mass payouts.
- **Icons**: a Featured Icon with a key in the header and on each key (Warning, filled, as Figma's orange tile); the 3D key on the empty tab.
- **Test data**: our test logo, the owner from Members, an invented support link.

### Payment settings

- **Buttons, not switches.** Turning a setting on needs a currency, turning it off a question first; a Switch is for settings that take effect at once (the Switch rule), so the Figma *Turn on* and *Turn off* stay.
- **Fixed sum turns auto-convert on**, in the same currency, as the Figma toast says. While fixed sum is on, auto-convert can't be turned off: its button is unavailable and the row says *Turn off fixed sum invoice first*.
- **A currency first.** Nothing is chosen in *Turn on*, and the button waits for a choice. The coins sit before the names ([Radio](?path=/docs/components-radio--docs) label `picture`). The row shows the chosen currency.
- **Invoice fee, Referral accruals and Payment coverage** show their values; their *Change* buttons have no action in this demo. *Invoice fee* drops the product name.
