# Dashboard

The first page a business sees: how much it holds and in what, how payments are going, and what happened last. I designed it in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here it is rebuilt from the design system's components, and it works, together with the Assets page and the Convert and Withdraw flows.

**Try it:** point at the coloured bar or the chart; pick another period or any dates in the calendar; open **⋮** on Ethereum and convert; copy a transaction ID; press **View all** in Total balance to reach Assets, and **Dashboard** in the menu to come back. The sun in the bar switches the theme.

## What the page has to get right

- **The balance in one look.** The total, then how it splits: the bar shows the shares, the list names the four largest with their value in euros and in the coin.
- **Payments over a chosen period.** Today by the hour, this week by the day, this month, the last 30 days, last month, or any dates; the current hour or day stands out, and the sums for paid, pending and timed-out payments sit under the chart.
- **Act from where the money is.** Convert and withdraw from an asset's menu, without going to another page first.
- **Every part says when it is empty**, so a new account doesn't look broken.

## Decided while building it

- **The shares add up.** The percentages under each asset are its share of the total balance (Figma's tooltip on the bar shows the same number), counted from the balances on the Assets page, so the bar, the list and the total always agree. The bar colours the three largest and puts the rest in grey.
- **The same data as Assets.** The total is the Assets page's estimated balance, and a conversion or a withdrawal changes both pages.
- **The period is the system's picker.** The Figma list (Today, This week, This month, Last 30 days, Last month) became the presets of the period Filter with its Date Picker, so any other period can be picked in the calendar too. Figma draws *This month*; the others follow the same chart: one day by the hour, up to two months by the day, longer periods by the month. Clearing the period goes back to This month.
- **The chart and the bar work with the keyboard.** Each is one Tab stop; ← → move along the days or the parts, and each says its figures to screen readers.
- **The bar's tooltip is the system's Tooltip**, which gained Figma's small table for it: the coin and the share, then Amount and Balance under a line (the coin is 20px, the smallest Coin Icon).
- **Any width.** Full screen follows the screen: the sidebar folds into icons on tablets and into the menu on phones; the columns become one under 950px; on narrow cards the tools go under the title and every other day stays on the axis. On this page each example is a narrow window, so its sidebar is folded.
- **The verification modal has a Featured icon** where Figma has the trophy: the Modal has no place for a picture above the title yet.
- **Deposit comes later**, so it is unavailable in the menu; Withdraw works for ether, as in the Withdraw flow. Pages this demo doesn't have (Invoices, Transactions) stay in the menu without a destination.
- **Create new invoice by the page title.** In Figma it sat in the payments card, next to the period and View all; as the page's main action it moved up next to *Dashboard*, and every card keeps the same top: its title, then View all on the right.
- **Copying an ID** shows a short *Copied* Toast that goes by itself.
