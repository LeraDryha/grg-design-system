What the business holds, in each currency and in total, and where its money goes next: convert, deposit, withdraw. **Try it:** choose another currency by the estimated balance; hover the info button by *Withdrawal in progress*; **Hide small balances**; **Convert** or **Withdraw** from a row (an empty balance has them off).

## What the page has to get right

- **The total at a glance, in the currency people think in.** The estimated balance and the withdrawal in progress, in EUR or any of five currencies.
- **What isn't free to spend.** The withdrawal in progress is reserved; the info button says so.
- **Every asset and what can be done with it.** Amount, its value in EUR, and the three actions; an empty balance can still be topped up.
- **Less noise when needed.** Small balances (under 1 EUR) hide with one checkbox.

## Decided while building it

- **The currency of the balance is an Inline Select** by the number, as in Figma: BTC, ETH, USDT, USD, EUR. Both the estimated balance and the withdrawal in progress follow it; the table stays in EUR.
- **The info button is the blue Action Button with a tooltip**, with the Figma text: *Temporarily reserved amount of assets for withdrawal*.
- **Empty amounts follow [Formats](?path=/docs/foundations-formats--docs)**: *0*, not Figma's *0.00000000*.
- **Deposit has no screens yet**: the button stays without an action, as *Download* on Invoices.
- **Three equal columns** before the actions, as in Figma.
- **The page starts 24px from the sidebar** (after the check against Figma), on every In product page, as all the Figma frames do; the title row is 32px, with small buttons.
