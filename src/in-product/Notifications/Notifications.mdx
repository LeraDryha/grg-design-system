# Notifications

What happened to the business's money while nobody was looking: deposits, withdrawals, invoice payments, conversions, a new sign-in. I designed these screens in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here they are rebuilt from the design system's components, and they work: the notifications come from the demo's own transactions, and a new deposit arrives a few seconds after the page opens.

**Try it:** wait for the new deposit (the bell gets its dot), open it from the bell or the page: its transaction opens on Transactions. Choose a category, **Hide read**, **Mark all as read**, and **View all notifications** from the bell. The bell works the same on the Dashboard.

## What the page has to get right

- **The money first.** Every deposit, withdrawal, payment and conversion tells the amount, as in the transaction itself, so nobody has to open it to know.
- **Straight to the details.** A notification is a link: it opens its transaction and is read from then on.
- **What's new at a glance.** The red dot and the count stay until something is read; *Hide read* leaves only the new ones.

## Decided while building it

- **Version 2.** Figma has each notification with *View details* (version 1) or the whole row as the link (version 2). Version 2 is built: the Notification Feed rule says a notification is a link to its details, one control at a time.
- **Real notifications.** They are made from the demo's transactions (completed deposits, withdrawals, invoice payments and conversions, a cancelled withdrawal), with a new sign-in and the business verification; amounts and times match Transactions. A new ETH deposit arrives 8 seconds after the app opens.
- **No Payouts and Referrals categories:** the product has no such pages. The categories: All (the unread count), Sign-in, Invoices, Deposits, Withdrawals, Auto-conversion, Verification, with the Transaction icons' colours.
- **Settings later.** *Manage notifications* and the bell's settings have no action yet: the settings will live in the profile.
- **Empty** as the Notification Feed shows it: a Featured Icon with the bell, not the 3D picture.
- **Words:** *ETH deposit successful* (Figma: *successfully*), *Notifications* and *View all notifications*.
