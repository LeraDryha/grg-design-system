# Transactions

Every movement of a business's money in one list: invoice payments, deposits, withdrawals, exchanges and referral cashback. I designed these screens in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here they are rebuilt from the design system's components, and they work.

**Try it:** filter by type, status, currency or period, then **Reset**; search by an ID or a blockchain ID. Open a row for its details, add or edit the comment on a withdrawal, and **Open as a page**.

## What the flow has to get right

- **Tell the kinds apart at a glance.** Each kind has its colour and icon, the same in the list, the filters and the details; a transaction that didn't go through turns red with a cross.
- **Find one transaction fast.** Search by its ID or its blockchain ID, or narrow the list by type, status, currency and period.
- **Everything about it in one place.** The details show what matters for its kind: the invoice it paid, the network and the addresses, the card, the exchange rate, the fee.
- **Notes for the team.** A withdrawal takes a comment, so others know why it was made or cancelled.

## Decided while building it

- **Details in a drawer, and as a page.** Figma opened every transaction as a page. A drawer keeps the list in view (as on Invoices); *Open as a page* shows the Figma page.
- **The colour moved into the drawer's header.** Figma put a coloured band above the details card. Here the transaction's colour fills the drawer's header, behind the title, the icon, the amount and the status (Drawer `tint` and `lead`); on the page, the same block is a tinted box. Invoices take the colour of their status the same way.
- **One state in the icon: failed.** A cancelled or arrested transaction is red with a cross, as in Figma; every other status stays in the Badge, so the colour keeps meaning the kind (Transaction Icon `failed`).
- **Referral cashback got its own kind**: purple, with a gift (it was in the Figma type list without an icon).
- **The comment is written in place.** Figma opened a modal for it; a modal over a drawer breaks the rule, so the pencil opens the field right there, with *Cancel* and *Save*.
- **Filters right after the search, then Reset**, as on Invoices. The period opens the whole Date Picker with its presets.
- **Refunded is in the status list.** The Figma table shows it; the Figma list didn't have it.
- **Convert is gone** from the details: it had no clear job there.
- **An exchange shows both currencies** (“0.03856091 ETH → EUR”), in the list and in the details, where Figma's header showed a different amount.
