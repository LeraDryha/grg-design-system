# Invoices

A business sells by link: it creates an invoice, sends the link to its customer and follows the payment. I designed these screens in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here they are rebuilt from the design system's components, and they work.

**Try it:** filter by asset, status or period; open a paid invoice, its *Operations*, and *View in transactions*; press **Create invoice**, type an amount, add a custom field and create it; copy the link, then **View invoice**. Open a row for its details, **Deactivate link** from the drawer or from a row, and **Open as a page**.

## What the flow has to get right

- **A link in seconds.** Only the currency and the amount are required; everything else is optional and folds away.
- **The link right after.** The new invoice shows its amount, status and link to copy without leaving the list.
- **Follow every payment.** Each invoice's details and operations are a click away from its row, and as a page for the full story.
- **Stop a link safely.** Deactivating asks first and says what it means: nobody can pay with it any more.

## Decided while building it

- **Creating moved into a drawer.** Figma made Create invoice a page. In a drawer the list stays in view, the new invoice appears in it, and creating, its result, the details and deactivating are one drawer whose content changes (the Drawer rule: one task, one drawer).
- **The result stays in the drawer**, with the amount, the status and the link to copy, then *View invoice* or *Create another*.
- **Details in the drawer and as a page.** A row opens the details in the drawer (*Details* and *Operations*); *Open as a page* shows the Figma page, for the full history.
- **The status gives the colour.** The drawer's header takes the colour of the invoice's status, with the amount and the Badge, and its icon is drawn the way Transactions draw a kind (Transaction Icon): amber with a clock while it waits, green with a check when paid, red with a cross when it timed out or was deactivated. On the page, the same block is a tinted box. Transactions do the same with their kind's colour.
- **Details on grey cards, as in Figma**: the figures, the link and the list of details each sit on a grey card (bg-muted-50, 16px corners). The drawer has the page's surface, so they look the same in it, in both themes.
- **Operations as in Figma.** Each payment, and its auto-conversion when it is on, is a grey card with its kind, status, amount and time, and *View in transactions* under it: it opens that transaction on the Transactions page, in its drawer.
- **Custom fields are typed in the drawer.** Figma added them in a modal; a modal over a drawer breaks the rule, so the field opens in place, with edit and remove for each custom field.
- **Deactivate asks where it is.** From a row or the page, the Figma modal; from the drawer, the same question inside the drawer.
- **Filters right after the search, then Reset.** Figma put the search on the left and the filters on the right; here they follow the search, so more filters can join the row, and *Reset* appears as soon as anything is searched or chosen.
- **The filters are selects.** Asset and status are the GRX Pay selects; the period is a select too, which opens the whole Date Picker, as in Figma: its presets (All period, Today, Last 7 days…) and the calendar with Reset and Apply.
- **The optional sections fold away.** *Redirect link* starts folded, *Customer details* open.
- **Before verification**, the Figma empty states with the system's Featured icon in place of the 3D pictures.
- **Download stays without an action** in this demo; the payment links use `pay.example.com`.
