# Convert

A business swaps one asset for another: ether for euros, euros for a stablecoin. I designed this flow in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here it is rebuilt from the design system's components, and it works.

**Try it:** press **Convert** on the Ethereum row. Type what you spend in **From**, or exactly what you want to receive in **To**; or press **75%**. Change either coin in its select. Confirm, and both balances change in the table.

## What the flow has to get right

- **Both sides at once.** What leaves, what arrives and at what rate are on screen before anything is pressed.
- **Type the number you know.** Some people know what they want to spend, others exactly what they need to receive; either field takes the amount and the other follows.
- **No dead ends.** Every error says what is wrong and what is available, and the button waits until the amount works.

## Design decisions

- **Two linked boxes.** From sits over To with an arrow between them; a minus and a plus show what leaves the balance and what arrives.
- **The balance warns first.** When the amount is more than the balance, the balance in the box turns red and *Over balance* appears under the amount.
- **Shares of the balance.** 25%, 50%, 75% and MAX fill From in one press; the chosen one is checked until the amount is typed by hand.
- **The market has a say.** When the market can't take the whole amount, the modal says so and how much it can take.
- **A confirmation step.** It repeats the amount, the rate and the fee, and the button repeats the amount: *Convert ~ 0.62 ETH*.
- **Two endings.** *Order successfully submitted*, or *Oops, the transaction failed* with a support code to copy and a way to the support team.

## Decided while building it

<Canvas of={ConvertStories.NotEnoughOnTheMarket} />

- **The limits moved into the modal.** Fee, minimum and maximum sat in a popover beside the modal, as in Withdraw; they are now a card above the Convert button, red when the amount is outside them.
- **Coins from a dropdown, not a separate window.** The first design opened *You receive*, a window with a search and a list of coins. The select now opens the system's dropdown list with a search, in place: one step less, and the amounts stay in view. Choosing the coin of the other side swaps the two.
- **Test data.** Only ETH to USDC is limited by the market (1.45866 ETH), to show that message. *Contact the support team* has no page behind it in this demo.
