# Withdraw ETH

A business sends crypto out of its account: from the Assets page to a payment on its way. I designed this flow in Figma for a crypto payments platform; here it is rebuilt from the design system's components, and it works.

**Try it:** press **Withdraw** on the Ethereum row, choose **Test**, type an amount (or press **MAX**), add a comment, switch on **Save recipient** and follow it to the end. The Ethereum balance goes down when you are done. Only Withdraw is wired up; the rest of the page is still.

## What the flow has to get right

- **No surprises about money.** The fee and the total are on screen before anything is sent, and once more on the last check.
- **Few questions, one at a time.** Method, amount, check, send: each step asks one thing, and the back arrow returns without losing what was typed.
- **Next time is faster.** A recipient can be saved at the end, when the person has seen everything about the payment.

## Design decisions

- **The amount is the hero.** It is typed large in the middle of the modal, with the coin beside it. **MAX** fills in the most that can be sent once the fee is taken off.
- **Methods are cards, not a dropdown.** Each shows its fee, so the choice and its cost are read together, in one tap.
- **The comment is optional and folded away.** *Add* opens it in its own step; back on the amount it shows in full with *Edit*.
- **Two endings.** *Payment processing* while the provider takes the payment, then *Transaction in progress*, honest that the network can take time.

## Decided while building it

<Canvas of={WithdrawStories.OverTheLimit} />

- **The limit moved into the modal.** The maximum first sat in a popover beside the modal: easy to miss, with no room for it on a phone, and screen readers wouldn't connect it to the amount. It is now a card in the modal, under MAX (the Figma frame was updated too). When the amount goes over the maximum or the balance, the numbers and the card turn red and the note under the amount says why; Continue waits until the amount works.
- **The amount takes digits and one point**; a comma counts as the point.
- **Every step is reachable by keyboard**: the modals keep focus inside, Escape closes them, and focus returns to the Withdraw button.
