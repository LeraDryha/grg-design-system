## Anatomy

<Canvas of={ActionButtonStories.Anatomy} />

1. **Container:** the button area, no fill or border. Only the icon colour changes between states.
2. **Icon:** use the standard Base icons; their size follows the button size. Social, country or coin icons need to be agreed first.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={ActionButtonStories.Sizes} />

From the left:

- **md-24:** 24 × 24 icon.
- **sm-20:** 20 × 20 icon. Inside fields, such as Input's copy and reveal buttons.
- **xs-16:** 16 × 16 icon. Next to small text, such as the info button of a field label.

The small sizes keep a 24 × 24 touch area around the icon.

## Colours

<Canvas of={ActionButtonStories.Colors} />

From the left:

- **Black:** utility actions like copy, edit, duplicate, more, and other non-primary interactions. In Figma and code this colour is called Grey.
- **Blue:** informational or context-specific actions where the colour reinforces the meaning: info, help, view details.
- **Red:** destructive actions: delete, remove.

## Accessibility

- The button has no visible text, so `aria-label` is required: say what happens, such as *Copy referral link* or *Delete row*, not the icon's name.
- The icon is decorative; the name comes from `aria-label`.
- Small sizes (sm-20, xs-16) keep a 24 × 24 touch area, the WCAG 2.2 minimum target size.
- Focus uses the system blue ring. Figma draws a light rounded frame around the icon, which keyboard users can barely see.
- A disabled action button is a native disabled button: skipped by Tab and announced as unavailable.
