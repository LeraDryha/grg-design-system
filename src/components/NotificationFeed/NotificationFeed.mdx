## Design decisions

- **A panel, not a page.** PrimeReact OverlayPanel: it is placed under the bell, closes on a click outside and on Escape. It is a dialog named *Notifications*.
- **400px wide, up to 640px tall**, as the guidelines say; the list scrolls under the header and the tabs.
- **A notification is a link** to its details; with buttons only the buttons act, so there is one control at a time.
- **The date follows the page's language**: *09/04/2026, 10:54:14* in British English, as in Figma.
- **New notifications are announced** while the feed is open (*1 new notification*), quietly, without moving focus. This is the ARIA live region, the standard way for content that changes on its own; PrimeReact has nothing for it.
- **Empty** uses a Featured Icon with the bell instead of the 3D bell picture.
- **Words.** The title is *Notifications* and the footer *View all notifications*; Figma has them singular (see [Contributing/Tokens](?path=/docs/contributing-tokens--docs)).

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Enter / Space on the bell | Opens the feed; focus moves to the selected tab |
| ← / → | Moves between All and Unread |
| Tab | Moves through the notifications and buttons |
| Escape | Closes the feed; focus goes back to the bell |

- The bell is named with the count (*Notifications, 3 unread*) and has `aria-expanded`.
- Unread notifications say *Unread* after their text.
- Known contrast from Figma: the date (`text-disabled-400`, 10px) is about 2.6:1, under 4.5:1. A darker grey such as `text-soft-500` would pass.
