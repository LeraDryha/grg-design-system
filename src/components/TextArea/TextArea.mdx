## Anatomy

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.Anatomy} />

1. **Container:** the field frame. Its border and fill show the state: hovered, active, error, disabled.
2. **Label:** what the field asks for. Inside the empty field it works as the placeholder, then floats above.
3. **Required asterisk:** the field must be filled before the form can be sent.
4. **Additional text (optional):** a short note after the label, such as *(Optional)*.
5. **Info button (optional):** more context about the field, without taking space in the form.
6. **Placeholder:** where the person types; shows the label or an example until there is a value.
7. **Icon:** marks the hint; turns red together with an error.
8. **Hint text (optional):** help or what to write. An error message replaces it.
9. **Top Key: Value (optional):** extra information about the field.
10. **Bottom Value (optional):** a value under the field.
11. **Character counter (optional):** the count and the limit, *153/200*. Its own component in Figma.
12. **Resize handle:** drag it to make the field taller or shorter. In Figma it is the *16/resize* icon inside the counter.

Parts 2–10 are the shared field parts, the same as in Input: see [Form Field](?path=/docs/components-form-field--docs).

## Sizes

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.Sizes} />

From the top:

- **lg (44px base input):** 12px paddings. The default, and the size for a standalone field such as a feedback section.
- **md (40px base input):** 10px paddings.
- **sm (32px base input):** 6px top and bottom, 8px on the sides.

Match the size to the Inputs next to it in a form. The text (Body-md 14/20), the label (Body-md Semibold 14/20), the 10px corners and the 122px starting height stay the same.
The counter sits 8px under the text; the label row is 2px above the field, the hint row 2px under it.

## Character counter

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.Counter} />

From the left:

- **Default:** the count and the limit, in the soft text colour.
- **Error:** over the limit, or when the field has an error. The field shows *Character limit exceeded.* until the text is short enough.
- **Disabled:** grey, with the field.

The limit is soft: people can type or paste past it and then shorten the text, instead of losing what they pasted.
Without `maxLength` the counter shows only the count.

## Resizing

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.Resizing} />

The icon at the bottom right is the **resize handle**: it shows that the field can grow down and shrink back.

- **Drag it** down to make the field taller, up to make it shorter. The cursor turns into a vertical resize arrow over it.
- **With the keyboard:** reach it with Tab, then ↓ and ↑ change the height by one line (20px), Home and End jump to the smallest and largest size.
- **Limits:** from the starting height (122px) to **400px**, about 14 lines of text. Past that the text scrolls inside the field, so a long message never pushes the rest of the form off the screen. A common practice is to stop at roughly half a laptop screen; Figma sets no maximum.
- The handle follows the field: red with an error, grey and inactive when the field is disabled. Turn it off with `resizable={false}`.

## Floating label

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.FloatingLabel} />

From the left:

1. **Empty.** The label is the placeholder: it sits inside the field, in the placeholder colour.
2. **Focused.** The label moves above the field and turns dark; the field is ready for typing.
3. **Filled.** The label stays above while the field has a value. Clearing the field brings it back inside.

The floating label **takes no vertical space**: leave 22px above every field in a form layout.

## Accessibility

- The label is a real `<label>` connected to the field, so it stays the field's name even while it is shown as the placeholder.
- The hint, the error and the counter are connected with `aria-describedby`. Screen readers hear *153 of 200 characters* with the field, not on every keystroke.
- An error, including going over the limit, sets `aria-invalid`.
- Focus is visible: the border turns dark in the Active state.
- The resize handle is a focusable separator named *Resize text area*, with a tooltip. The arrow keys resize the field, so it works without a mouse.
- Colours follow Figma. On the hovered field the counter (`text-soft-500` on `bg-weak-100`) reaches 4.3:1, just under the 4.5:1 for text.
