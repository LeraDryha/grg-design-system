# Drawer

A panel at the right edge of the screen for a task that belongs to the page under it: create an item, see its details. The page stays in view under the overlay.
It is made of Modal's parts: the same **header**, **content** and **footer**, the same overlay and the same rules, at the full height of the screen. On phones it takes the whole screen.

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Playground} />

```tsx
<Drawer
  visible={open}
  onHide={() => setOpen(false)}
  title="Create invoice"
  primaryAction={{ label: 'Create invoice', onClick: create }}
  secondaryAction={{ label: 'Cancel', onClick: () => setOpen(false) }}
>
  {fields}
</Drawer>
```

## Design decisions

- **Made of Modal's parts.** The Figma file has no drawer; the header, content, footer, overlay, spacing and rules are Modal's, so a drawer and a modal read as one family.
- **PrimeReact Dialog, not Sidebar.** Sidebar keeps no focus inside and isn't announced as a dialog. Dialog at the right edge gives the drawer what Modal has: focus kept inside, Escape (which first closes a list open in it), focus back to the control that opened it, the page behind not scrolling.
- **One task, one drawer.** Steps and results change inside the drawer; nothing opens a Modal or a second drawer over it. Lists inside it open over it.
- **Full height, the content scrolls.** The header and the footer stay; a line sets the footer apart from the scrolling content.
- **Square corners and 24px at the sides**, tighter than Modal's 32px; Close sits on the title's line; the footer has 16px above and below its buttons. It slides in from the right.
- **The item's colour in the header** (`tint`, `lead`). Details of a transaction or an invoice show what the item is under the title, its icon, amount and status, on its colour (`--add-<colour>-50` for a kind, `--<status>-muted-50` for a status). The header stays while the content scrolls, so the item stays named. A drawer with a form keeps a white header.
- **The content is never wider than the drawer** (the check after every story covers drawers too).

## Sizes

<Canvas of={DrawerStories.Sizes} />

- **sm-480**: a form, such as Create invoice.
- **md-560**: the details of an item.
- **lg-960**: a form with a preview beside it.
- **Phones** (under 480px): the whole screen, with the mobile header and footer of Modal.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab / Shift+Tab | Moves through the drawer; focus stays inside |
| Escape | Closes an open list first, then the drawer; focus goes back to what opened it |
| Enter / Space | Activates the focused button |

- The drawer is a dialog (`aria-modal`), named by its title and described by the line under it.
- Focus starts in the first field, otherwise on the main action, otherwise on the first button.
