## Design decisions

- **Black turns light in the dark theme**, so it still stands out on the dark sidebar.
- **Primary keeps its gold and dark text in both themes**: white text on gold would be about 2:1.
- **The Primary Support border** is the card's own gold (`primary-gold-base`); Figma uses a palette colour (see [Contributing/Tokens](?path=/docs/contributing-tokens--docs)).
- **No PrimeReact base**: a link, a button or a plain block.

## Colors

<Canvas of={FeatureCardStories.Colors} />

- **White** and **Gray** for calm screens, **Black** to stand out, **Primary** for the brand's own offers.
- **Left icon**: a Neutral [Featured Icon](?path=/docs/components-featured-icon--docs) (sm-32) and a line. **Support**: an icon before the title, two lines and a close button.

## Accessibility

- Left icon is a single link (or a button with `onClick` only); its name is the title and the line.
- The close button is named *Close* (`closeLabel`).
