The bar at the top of an app: the logo, the pages or the company, the language, the theme, a few icon buttons and the account. On tablets and phones the pages, the settings and the account move into a menu under the bar.

```tsx
<TopbarNavigation
  type="navigation"
  logo={<a href="/">…</a>}
  sections={[{ items: [{ id: 'dashboard', label: 'Dashboard', href: '/dashboard' }] }]}
  current={page}
  theme={theme}
  onThemeChange={setTheme}
```

## Design decisions

What the code adds to the Figma file, and why:

- **Types are named by what they hold**: `navigation` (Figma Exchange) and `company` (Figma Pay), not by the products.
- **The logo is the product's**: the GRX logos are not shown in this Storybook; the examples use a neutral one.
- **Sizes follow the screen** (`layout="auto"`): Desktop from 1024px, Tablet 768–1023px, Mobile below. Sidebar Navigation hides under 1024px too, so the pages are in one place.
- **The content of type navigation is centred in 1180px**, at least 24px from the edges: Figma draws 130px sides at 1440px, without a token.
- **The active page is semibold**, as in Sidebar Navigation; Figma keeps it Medium in the bar (see [Contributing/Tokens](?path=/docs/contributing-tokens--docs)).
- **The mobile menu** comes from the Figma examples: under the bar, the full width. Help and Settings move into it under a line; Notifications (`inBar`) stays in the bar until the menu opens.
- **The theme button** shows a moon in the light theme and a sun in the dark one; screen readers hear *Dark theme, pressed / not pressed*. In the menu it is the Theme switch.
- **Icon buttons** have tooltips with their names; the red dot is read with the name (*Notifications, new*). An action can bring its own element, such as the [Notification Feed](?path=/docs/components-notification-feed--docs) with a `TopbarButton` as its bell.
- **Page icons** are off in the bar, as the Figma bars draw them (`itemIcons` turns them on); the mobile menu shows them, like the sidebar.
- **The bar stays at the top** while the page scrolls.
- **Built from plain links**, like Sidebar Navigation; PrimeReact gives the menus (TieredMenu), the language list (Dropdown, through Inline Select) and the tooltips.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={TopbarStories.Sizes} />

- **Desktop**: everything in the bar. Type navigation: the logo and the pages on the left; type company: the logo and the company card.
- **Tablet** (24px sides) and **Mobile** (16px sides): the logo, the buttons that stay (`inBar`) and the menu button.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab / Shift + Tab | Moves between the logo, the pages, the language, the buttons and the account |
| Enter / Space | Opens a page, a menu or the language list |
| Escape | Closes a menu, the language list or the mobile menu |

- The bar is a `header`; the pages are a `nav` named *Main*.
- The menu button has `aria-expanded` and names the menu it opens; the theme button has `aria-pressed`.
- Icon buttons are named by their label, with the badge when there is one.
