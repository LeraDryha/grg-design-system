## When to use

## Anatomy

<Canvas of={Stories.Anatomy} />

1. **Container:** a button with a 1px stroke and corners defined by its Side.
2. **Icon:** an optional Base icon, separated from the label by 6px.
3. **Label:** Semibold text; `label` also names an icon-only button.

## Types

<Canvas of={Stories.Types} />

From the left: **Text only**, **Text + icon** and **Icon only**.

<Canvas of={Stories.Icons} />

Left and right icons can be chosen independently. The standalone item defaults to plus icons on both sides, as in Figma. Icon-only uses the left icon, then the right icon, with plus as the fallback.

### Sides

<Canvas of={Stories.Sides} />

**Left** rounds the left corners, **Center** has square corners and **Right** rounds the right corners. The assembled group assigns these automatically. **Single** rounds all corners for a one-item group.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={Stories.Sizes} />

- **md-32:** 32px high, 14/20 Semibold, 20px icons, 6×12px padding and 8px rounded corners.
- **sm-24:** 24px high, 12/16 Semibold, 16px icons, 4×8px padding and 6px rounded corners.

Icon-only items are square. The actual bound component styles take precedence over the guidelines' Medium label and md 6px radius descriptions.

## Behaviour

The standalone item uses PrimeReact Button. Pass `selected` and update it in `onClick` for a controlled toggle; use Button group for single selection and arrow navigation. The default `type="button"` avoids submitting a surrounding form; `submit` and `reset` are available explicitly. `previewState` exists for static documentation; normal buttons respond to hover and press automatically.

## Accessibility

Tab reaches each enabled button; Enter and Space activate it. The visible label names the button. Icon-only buttons use `label`, or an explicit `aria-label` when more context is needed. Icons are decorative. Native disabled buttons cannot be activated. Keyboard focus uses `var(--focus-ring)`.
