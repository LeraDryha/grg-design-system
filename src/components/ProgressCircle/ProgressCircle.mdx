While the length isn't known, leave out `value`: PrimeReact's spinner turns a quarter of the ring on the track. With reduced motion it turns slowly.

## Design decisions

- **Any value from 0 to 100.** Figma has steps of 25% because of its variants.
- **Drawn here for a known value**: PrimeReact only has a turning spinner (used for the unknown length, sized to the Figma ring).
- **One colour**, Primary, as in Figma.
- **No number at xs-24**: there is no room for it.
- **Label styles**: lg Body-md/Medium (the guidelines say *Body-mb*, a typo; see [Contributing/Tokens](?path=/docs/contributing-tokens--docs)), md Body-sm/Medium, sm Caption-xs/Medium.

## Sizes and values

<Canvas of={CircleStories.SizesAndValues} />

lg-64 (4px stroke), md-48 (4px), sm-32 (3px), xs-24 (2px).

## Accessibility

- A `progressbar` with `aria-valuenow`, named by `aria-label` (required: the ring has no visible label).
- The number inside is hidden from screen readers, so it isn't read twice.
