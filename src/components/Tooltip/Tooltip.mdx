A short text that appears next to a control on hover and on keyboard focus: the name of an icon button, or a hint about a setting.

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Playground} />
<Controls of={TooltipStories.Playground} include={['title', 'description', 'icon', 'color', 'side', 'align', 'purpose']} />

```tsx
<Tooltip title="Copy link" purpose="name">
  <ActionButton icon="copy" aria-label="Copy link" />
</Tooltip>
```

## When to use

## Design decisions

- **Hover and keyboard focus** both show it, after 150ms; it stays while the pointer moves onto it, and Escape closes it without moving focus (WCAG 1.4.13).
- **Screen readers.** PrimeReact hides the tooltip from them, so its text reaches them through the control: as its name, its description, or not at all when the control already says it (`purpose`).
- **`side` is where the tooltip opens.** Figma Side names the edge with the tip, which is the opposite: Side = Bottom is a tooltip above the control (`side="top"`). Top left / right and Bottom left / right are `align="start"` / `"end"`.
- **It moves when there is no room**, to the opposite side.
- **Black turns light in the dark theme**, as its tokens (`bg-base-1000`, `text-inverce`) do.

## Sides

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Sides} />

Hover each button. `top` is the default; tooltips in a collapsed sidebar open to the `right`.

## Styles

<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Styles} />

- **White** for most screens; **Black** (Figma Inverted) to stand out on busy content.
- A title alone (6px corners), with a Left icon, or a title with a description (Details = On: 12px corners, up to 301px wide).

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Focus on the control shows the tooltip |
| Escape | Hides it; focus stays on the control |

- The text is part of the control: its name (`purpose="name"`) or description (`purpose="description"`).
- Only controls that take focus get a tooltip, so keyboard users see it too.
