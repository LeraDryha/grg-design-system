A field that opens a list to choose one option: a payment type, a country, a user, a payment method, a coin.
It shares the label, hint and states with Input; the type sets the picture before the value.

## Anatomy

<Canvas of={SelectStories.Anatomy} />

1. **Container:** the field frame. Its border and fill show the state: hovered, active, error, disabled.
2. **Label:** what the field asks for. Inside the empty field it works as the placeholder, then floats above.
3. **Required asterisk:** a choice is needed before the form can be sent.
4. **Additional text (optional):** a short note after the label, such as *(Optional)*.
5. **Info button (optional):** more context about the field.
6. **Placeholder:** the chosen option, or the label or a placeholder until there is one.
7. **Icon:** marks the hint; turns red together with an error.
8. **Hint text (optional):** help about the choice. An error message replaces it.
9. **Picture (optional, Figma Icon):** 20 × 20, set by the type: a Base icon, a flag, a photo, a payment card (28 × 20) or a coin. An empty field shows a placeholder icon: coin, globe, user or card.
10. **Chevron:** points down, up while the list is open.

Parts 2–8 are the shared field parts, the same as in Input: see [Form Field](?path=/docs/components-form-field--docs).

## Types

<Canvas of={SelectStories.Types} />

From the top:

- **Common:** any list, with a Base icon per option or one icon for the field. Payment type, category.
- **Country:** a flag from [Country icons](?path=/docs/foundations-icons-country-icons--docs).
- **User:** an [Avatar](?path=/docs/components-avatar--docs) at 20px: a photo, initials or a person or company shape.
- **Payment method:** a card from [Payment method icons](?path=/docs/foundations-icons-payment-method-icons--docs), 28 × 20.
- **Coin:** a logo from [Coin icons](?path=/docs/foundations-icons-coin-icons--docs).

## Sizes

<Canvas of={SelectStories.Sizes} />

From the top:

- **lg-44:** 12px paddings, 10px corners. Forms with few fields.
- **md-40:** 10px paddings, 10px corners. The default for product forms.
- **sm-32:** 6px top and bottom, 8px on the sides, 8px corners. Dense screens: filters, tables.

The label (Body-md Semibold 14/20), the value (Body-md 14/20) and the 20px picture stay the same in every size.
The label row is 2px above the field, the hint row 2px under it.

## Open list

<Canvas of={SelectStories.OpenList} />

The list opens 8px under the field, on the floating surface with a small shadow, 12px corners and 8px padding (Figma Dropdown).

- **Rows** (Dropdown menu item): 40px, the option's picture and name; a light fill on hover and while moving with the arrow keys; a check on the chosen option.
- **Search** (`searchable`): a row at the top that filters the list as people type, with a clear button. Use it for long lists, as in the Figma examples for countries, users and coins.
- After about six rows the list scrolls.

The list is the start of the Dropdown menu component, which Figma also uses for menus; descriptions, badges and checkboxes come with it.

## Floating label

<Canvas of={SelectStories.FloatingLabel} />

From the left:

1. **Empty.** The label is the placeholder: it sits inside the field, in the placeholder colour.
2. **Focused or open.** The label moves above the field and turns dark.
3. **Filled.** The label stays above while the field has a value.

The floating label **takes no vertical space**: leave 22px above every field in a form layout.

## Multi-select

The Figma examples also show a multi-select: options with checkboxes, the chosen ones listed in the field with commas
(*Payman, Stripe, Mastercard Gateway, Crypto Processing and 2 others*) and a clear button. It needs Checkbox and the
Dropdown menu with checkbox rows, so it comes after them.

## Accessibility

- The field is a combobox named by its label, with an expanded state and the highlighted option announced while moving with the arrow keys.
- Enter, Space or ↓ open the list; ↑ ↓ move, Enter chooses, Escape closes. With search, typing filters the list.
- The hint and the error are connected with `aria-describedby`; an error also sets `aria-invalid`.
- Pictures are decorative: the option names say what is chosen.
- Focus is visible: the border turns dark in the Active state.
