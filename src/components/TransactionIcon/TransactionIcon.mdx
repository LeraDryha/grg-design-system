## Design decisions

- **Four kinds** from the Figma examples: Deposit (blue, arrow down), Withdrawal (orange, arrow up), Payment (indigo, bill), Exchange (green). Figma sets the colour and the icon on each instance.
- **Decorative by default**; `label` makes it an image with a name.

## Types and sizes

<Canvas of={TIStories.TypesAndSizes} />

sm-20 in tables and tabs, md-24 in notifications, lg-48 on a transaction's own page.

## Accessibility

- Hidden from screen readers unless `label` is given.
- The colour is never the only cue: the icon and the text next to it say the kind.
