One choice out of a few, all visible at once. `RadioGroup` gives the options a title, a hint and an error; `RadioLabel` adds a label, a description and a link to each option.

<Canvas of={RadioStories.Playground} />
<Controls of={RadioStories.Playground} include={['size', 'destructive', 'disabled']} />

```tsx
<RadioGroup label="Payout method" value={method} onChange={setMethod}>
  <RadioLabel value="standard" label="Standard" sublabel="(Free)" description="1–3 business days." />
  <RadioLabel value="instant" label="Instant" sublabel="(1%)" description="Within 30 minutes." />
</RadioGroup>
```

## Design decisions

- **The same rules as Checkbox**: the native control (PrimeReact RadioButton) under the Figma shape, the label layout of Checkbox Label, a red error under the group with the same Hint text.
- **RadioGroup** is not in Figma: a `fieldset` with its title (Body-md/Medium), a hint, the options 12px apart (24px in a row) and the error.
- **A choice made beforehand** is the product's call: make it when one option is safe and common, leave it empty when people must decide.
- **Focus** uses the system blue ring.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Moves into the group (to the chosen option) and out of it |
| ↑ / ↓ (← / →) | Moves to the next option and chooses it |
| Space | Chooses the focused option |

- The group is a `fieldset` named by its title; its hint and error are read with each option.
- Known contrast from Figma: disabled options are light (as in every disabled control).
