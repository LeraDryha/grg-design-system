## Building a menu

Following the Figma guidelines *Combining and placing dropdown items*:

- Rows are stacked with **2px** between them, in a panel with **8px** padding, **12px** corners, `surface-floating` and the small elevation; rows fill the width.
- The menu opens **8px** under its trigger.
- Up to **9 rows** show without scrolling; with 10 or more the list scrolls.
- Extra parts (Figma *Dropdown misc items*): **divider line**, **divider text** (a section title), **caption** with a text button, and a full-width **button**. The **search** part belongs to the lists of choices (Select, Compact Select, Inline Select, Filter): a menu holds actions, and a list long enough to search is a choice, so it is a Select.

## Design decisions

- **One row for every list.** Dropdown Menu, Select, Compact Select and Inline Select share the Figma *Dropdown menu item*, so a fix reaches all of them.
- **Rows keep their height.** Every row without a description is 40px, with or without the check and with a checkbox too (Figma draws checkbox rows 36px), so choosing a row never makes it jump.
- **Rows named by all their text.** A row with a description or a badge is announced with all of it (*Free Plan, 12,000 views, Upgrade*), so what people see is what a screen reader says and what voice control finds.
- **Keyboard from PrimeReact.** TieredMenu provides the arrow keys and submenus; custom rows keep its activation, so Enter and Space work on every row.

## The row

<Canvas of={DropdownMenuStories.ItemOptions} />

A row (Dropdown menu item) has a **left picture** (a Base icon, coin, flag, payment card, avatar or social logo, 20px; an avatar with a description 40px), a **label** (Body-md 14/20) with an optional **description**, and on the right a **check** (single choice), a **checkbox** (multiple choice) or an **arrow** (submenu), with an optional **badge** before it.

## Item types

<Canvas of={DropdownMenuStories.ItemTypes} />

From the top: **Basic** (text only, 10px top and bottom), **Icon**, **Currency**, **Country**, **Payment method**, **Avatar** and **Social**.

## Item states

<Canvas of={DropdownMenuStories.ItemStates} />

- **Default** and **Hovered** (a light `bg-weak-100` fill, also on the row under the arrow keys).
- **Selected:** a check, or a checked checkbox in a multiple choice.
- **Disabled:** grey text, a faded picture; it can't be chosen and the arrow keys skip it.

## Accessibility

- The trigger says it opens a menu (`aria-haspopup`, `aria-expanded`); the menu has a name (`aria-label`).
- Enter, Space or ↓ open it; ↑ ↓ move, → opens a submenu and ← closes it, Enter chooses, Escape closes and returns to the trigger.
- Section titles and the header are not focusable; disabled rows are skipped.
- Pictures are decorative: the labels name the actions.
