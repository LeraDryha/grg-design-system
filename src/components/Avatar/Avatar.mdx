A picture of a person or a company: a photo, initials or a placeholder shape, with an optional status.
Used in profiles, lists, comments and the User type of Select.

## Anatomy

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Anatomy} />

1. **Image:** a photo or logo, initials, or a person or company shape, in a circle.
2. **Status (optional):** the Status badge at the bottom right, with a 2px ring in the surface colour.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Sizes} />

Eleven sizes from 120 to 16px. The status badge follows the size:

- **120 / 96 / 80:** Status badge md-24.
- **64 / 56:** sm-20.
- **48:** xs-16.
- **40 / 32:** xxs-12.
- **24:** mini-8, a dot without an icon.
- **20 / 16:** no badge.

Initials are bold and scale with the size: Heading H5 at 120, Title at 96–64, 14px at 56 and 48, 12px at 40, 11px at 32 and 10px below, with one letter at 20 and 16.

## Kinds

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Kinds} />

From the left:

- **Photo** (`src`): a photo or a company logo, cropped to the circle. If it fails to load, initials (when enabled) or the shape show instead.
- **Person** (`shape="user"`, the default): a placeholder before there is a photo.
- **Company** (`shape="company"`): the same for an organisation.
- **Initials** (`initials` with a `name`): the first letters of the first and last name.

### Photo recovery

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.PhotoRecovery} />

PrimeReact handles the photo, initials and shape fallback. A new photo URL starts a new loading attempt after a previous failure.

## Colours

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Colours} />

Grey by default; or one of the fourteen Additional colour groups (`color`). Both shades always come from the same group:

- **Shapes:** the 400 shade in the background, the shape in 50 (Sky 400 + Sky 50).
- **Initials:** the 300 shade in the background, the letters in 900 (Sky 300 + Sky 900).

Never mix groups, such as Yellow 50 with Purple 400. In the dark theme the shades swap places, so the avatar keeps its contrast.

## Status

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Statuses} />

From the left: **none**, **Check** (green), **Pending** (amber), **Remove** (red, a cross), **Error** (red, a minus) and **Icon** (a grey pencil, for an avatar that can be changed).

### Status badge

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.StatusBadges} />

Its own small component in Figma: five types in five sizes (24, 20, 16, 12 and 8). At 8px it is a dot without an icon.
Avatar picks the size; use it on its own only where Avatar can't be used.

## Accessibility

- With a `name`, the avatar is an image with that name, and the status is added to it: *Warren Fritsch, verified*. Change the status words with `statusLabel`.
- Without a name, or with `decorative` when the name is written next to it (lists, selects), screen readers skip it.
- The status is not shown by colour alone: each type has its own icon from 12px up.
- The edit badge is a picture, not a button: put the action on a button next to the avatar or on the avatar's container.
