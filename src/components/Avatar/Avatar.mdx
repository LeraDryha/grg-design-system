A picture of a person or a company: a photo, initials or a placeholder shape, with an optional status.
Used in profiles, lists, comments and the User type of Select.

## Examples

Every example is a live story. Its steps also run as a test in the **Interactions** tab.

### A list of people

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.AListOfPeople} />

When the name is written next to the avatar (lists, selects), `decorative` hides the picture from screen readers, so the name isn't read twice.

### A verified account

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.AVerifiedAccount} />

On its own, the avatar is an image named by the person and the status: *Warren Fritsch, verified*. Change the status words with `statusLabel`.

### A photo that fails

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.APhotoThatFails} />

If the photo can't load, initials (when enabled) or the shape show instead. A new photo address starts a new attempt.

## Design decisions

- **One colour group per avatar.** Shapes use the 400 shade with the shape in 50 (Sky 400 + Sky 50); initials use 300 with the letters in 900 (Sky 300 + Sky 900). The code takes both shades from one group, so they can't be mixed. In the dark theme the shades swap places and the avatar keeps its contrast.
- **The badge follows the size.** Avatar picks the Status badge size itself (24 at 120–80, 20 at 64–56, 16 at 48, 12 at 40–32, an 8px dot at 24, none at 20 and 16).
- **Status is not colour alone.** Every status has its own icon from 12px up.
- **The edit badge is a picture, not a button.** Put the action on a button next to the avatar or on its container.

## Kinds

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Kinds} />

A **photo** or a company logo (`src`), a **person** or **company** placeholder (`shape`), or **initials** from the `name`.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Sizes} />

Initials are bold and scale with the size: Heading H5 at 120, Title at 96–64, 14px at 56 and 48, 12px at 40, 11px at 32 and 10px below, one letter at 20 and 16.

## Colours

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Colours} />

Grey by default, or one of the fourteen Additional colour groups (`color`). Keep the same group for a person or company everywhere they appear.

## Status

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.Statuses} />

**Check** (green), **Pending** (amber), **Remove** (red, a cross), **Error** (red, a minus) and **Icon** (a grey pencil, for an avatar that can be changed).

<Canvas of={AvatarStories.StatusBadges} />

The Status badge is its own small component in Figma: five types in five sizes. Use it on its own only where Avatar can't be used.

## Accessibility

- With a `name`, the avatar is an image with that name and its status. Without a name, or with `decorative`, screen readers skip it.
- Don't put a status badge at 16 or 20px: there is no room for it.
