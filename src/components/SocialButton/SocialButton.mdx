## Anatomy

<Canvas of={SocialButtonStories.Anatomy} />

1. **Container:** a 44px tall frame with a 1px border; the border carries the state.
2. **Logo:** the Brand social icon, 24px. Google and Facebook keep their colours; the black Apple logo turns white on the dark theme.
3. **Button label:** *Sign in with Google* by default, as in Figma. Change it where the action differs: *Sign up with Google*, *Continue with Apple*.

## Accessibility

- It is a real button: screen readers call it a button, Enter and Space press it.
- The label names the account and the action. A logo-only button gets the same label as its accessible name.
- The logo is decorative: screen readers skip it, the label says which account it is.
- Focus uses the system blue ring.
- Figma has no disabled state: sign-in with an account is either offered or not shown.
