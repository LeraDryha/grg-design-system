## Examples

### Sign up

### Logo only

<Canvas of={SocialButtonStories.LogoOnly} />

A 44 × 44 square (Figma *Icon button*). The label isn't visible, but screen readers still hear it as the button's name.

## Design decisions

- **Logos keep their brand colours.** Google and Facebook keep theirs; the black Apple logo turns white on the dark theme, so it stays visible.
- **The logo is decorative.** Screen readers skip it; the label says which account it is.
- **No disabled state.** Figma has none: an account that can't be used is left out of the list rather than shown greyed. While sign-in is in progress, the product shows that on the page.
- **States are behaviour, not props.** The border darkens on hover (`stroke-strong-500`) and press (`stroke-stronger-900`); keyboard focus shows the system blue ring.

## Accounts

<Canvas of={SocialButtonStories.Accounts} />

Show all supported accounts together, in the same content and width. Don't mix buttons with and without labels in one group.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Moves focus to the button; the blue ring shows |
| Enter or Space | Presses the button |

- It is a real button: screen readers call it a button.
- The label names the account and the action. A logo-only button gets the same label as its name.
