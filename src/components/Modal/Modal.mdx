A window over the page for one short task or decision: confirm, choose, enter a code. The page behind waits under a dark overlay until the modal is closed.
It is built from a **header** (status icon, title, description, back and close), the **content**, and a **footer** with the actions. On phones it becomes a bottom sheet.

<Canvas of={ModalStories.Playground} />
<Controls of={ModalStories.Playground} include={['title', 'description', 'align', 'status', 'size', 'height', 'footerType', 'destructive', 'layout', 'dismissableMask']} />

```tsx
<Modal
  visible={open}
  onHide={() => setOpen(false)}
  title="Delete invoice?"
  description="You won’t be able to undo this."
  status="error"
  destructive
  footerType="fill"
  primaryAction={{ label: 'Delete', onClick: remove }}
  secondaryAction={{ label: 'Cancel', onClick: () => setOpen(false) }}
/>
```

## Design decisions

What the code adds to the Figma file, and why:

- **One component, parts inside.** Figma builds a modal from Modal header and Modal footer by hand. Here the props set both, and `ModalHeader`, `ModalFooter` and `ModalPanel` are exported for anything unusual.
- **12px between the parts of the content.** Cards, boxes, rows and notes in the content are 12px apart, and fields with floating labels 40px; a screen designed with other gaps (such as 24px in a flow) still follows the modal. The parts sit straight in the content, without wrappers of their own.
- **Spacing is automatic.** Figma asks the designer to switch the header's Padding bottom on with content and to add 20px above a first field. The modal does it from what is inside: on desktop 24px under the header when there is content, on mobile only when the content starts with a field.
- **Bottom sheet under 480px.** The Figma Mobile size, decided by the screen (`layout="auto"`); `layout` forces either one.
- **Focus.** It starts in the first field, otherwise on the safe action (Cancel when destructive, the main action otherwise), stays inside while the modal is open and goes back to the opening button when it closes. PrimeReact would focus its own close button.
- **Closing.** Close and Escape always; a click on the overlay only with `dismissableMask`, so nothing typed is lost by a stray click. The page behind doesn't scroll.
- **Never wider than the modal.** Every part of the content takes its width and can shrink to it, and nothing scrolls sideways. After every story a check fails if anything in a modal's content is wider than the modal.
- **Lists open over the modal.** A Select (or Compact Select, Inline Select, Filter) in the content opens its list over the modal instead of inside its scrolling content, where it was cut off; *Select user* shows it.
- **Overlay.** Figma draws #0A0A0A at 60% with an 8px blur, not as a variable; it is the `--overlay` token here (see [Contributing/Tokens](?path=/docs/contributing-tokens--docs)), the same in both themes.
- **Back arrow on Center aligned only**, as in Figma: in the other types it would sit on the status icon.
- **Not draggable, not resizable**, though PrimeReact Dialog is by default.
- **Motion.** A short fade and scale; the sheet slides up. Reduced motion keeps the fade only.

## Header types

<Canvas of={ModalStories.HeaderTypes} />

- **Center aligned:** confirmations and results; the only type with the back arrow.
- **Left aligned:** forms and settings, where the content is left aligned too.
- **Horizontal left aligned:** the icon next to the title, for long content (Full height).

The status icon is a [Featured Icon](?path=/docs/components-featured-icon--docs): Success, Info, Warning, Error, or Neutral and Primary with any Base icon.

## Footer types

<Canvas of={ModalStories.FooterTypes} />

- **Stretch:** two equal buttons side by side. **Fill:** full-width buttons, the main one on top (sm-440 modals).
- **Basic:** buttons on the right (md and lg modals).
- **Button, Link button, Stepper, Info, Checkbox:** Basic with one more item on the left, passed as `footerExtra`: a Text button, a Link, a Stepper Dot, a Hint text, a Checkbox label.
- **Destructive:** the main action in red (shown on Basic above).

<Canvas of={ModalStories.MobileFooters} />

On mobile every type is one column with the main action on top. The stepper goes above the buttons, the other items below.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={ModalStories.Sizes} />

sm-440 for confirmations and short forms, md-600 for forms with more fields and for tours, lg-880 for tables and previews. On phones the sheet takes the screen's width (xs-343 in Figma).

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab / Shift + Tab | Moves between the controls of the modal only |
| Escape | Closes the modal; focus goes back to what opened it |
| Enter / Space | Activates the focused button |

- The modal is a `dialog` with `aria-modal`, named by its title and described by its description.
- Back and Close are named *Back* and *Close* (`backLabel`, `closeLabel`).
- The page behind is inert for the keyboard and doesn't scroll while the modal is open.
