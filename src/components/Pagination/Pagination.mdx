## Design decisions

- **The numbers follow the Figma truncation**: the first and the last page always, the current page with its neighbours, "…" for the rest; near the ends the first or last three pages. A gap of a single page shows that page instead of "…". PrimeReact Paginator gives the controls and their names, but shows a sliding window without the ends.
- **First is on the outside**: `« ‹ … › »`, as most products and PrimeReact do; Figma draws `‹ «` (see [Contributing/Tokens](?path=/docs/contributing-tokens--docs)).
- **One page**: the pages disappear, as the guidelines say. The group keeps its counter and page size, so a larger page size can be changed back.
- **"…" isn't a control**: it only shows that pages are hidden.
- **The page size** is a Compact Select sm-32, 10 / 20 / 50 / 100 by default (`rowsOptions`).

## Types

<Canvas of={PaginationStories.Types} />

- **Default**: the arrows and the pages, for lists with their own counter.
- **Pagination group**: the counter on the left, the pages, the page size on the right; under tables.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Moves through the arrows, the pages and the page size |
| Enter / Space | Opens a page |

- The pagination is a `nav` named *Pagination* (`aria-label`); the current page has `aria-current="page"`.
- The arrows are named *First page*, *Previous page*, *Next page* and *Last page*; at the ends they are disabled.
- The counter is read out when it changes.
