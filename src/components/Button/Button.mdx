A button starts an action: submit a form, open a dialog, confirm a step.
Four types from the strongest to the quietest, five sizes, and colours for destructive and positive actions.

## Anatomy

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Anatomy} />

1. **Container:** the button's frame. Its fill and border carry the type, the colour and the state.
2. **Icon (optional):** before or after the label, or alone. Use the standard Base icons; their size follows the button size (16, 20 or 24px). Social, country or coin icons need to be agreed first.
3. **Button label:** a short verb that says what happens: *Save*, *Send invoice*, *Delete*.

## Types

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Types} />

From the left:

- **Primary:** the main action on a screen or in a dialog. One per area.
- **Secondary (default):** most actions. The type to use when nothing needs extra weight.
- **Outline:** alternative or less important actions next to a Primary one.
- **Text:** the quietest: inline actions, *Cancel*, actions in dense places such as tables and toolbars.

### Content

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Content} />

From the left: **text only**, **text with an icon** (before or after the label) and **icon only**. An icon-only button has no visible text, so it needs a name for screen readers.

### Colours

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Colors} />

From the top:

- **Primary colour:** every regular action.
- **Destructive (error) colour:** actions that delete, remove or cancel something for good.
- **Green (success) colour:** positive confirmations, such as approving a payment.

## Sizes

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Sizes} />

From the left:

- **xlg-56:** Body-lg/Semibold label (16/24), 24px icon. Hero actions on marketing pages.
- **lg-44:** Body-lg/Semibold label (16/24), 24px icon. Main actions in forms and dialogs.
- **md-40:** Body-md/Semibold label (14/20), 20px icon. The default in the product.
- **sm-32:** Body-md/Semibold label (14/20), 20px icon. Dense screens: toolbars, filters, cards.
- **xs-24:** Body-sm/Semibold label (12/16), 16px icon. Inside tables and small components.

The second row shows the icon-only button in each size: always square.

### Justified

<Canvas of={ButtonStories.Justified} />

By default a button is as wide as its label and icon. A justified button takes the full width of its container, for example the main action at the bottom of a form or on a phone.

## Accessibility

- The label is the button's name. An icon-only button needs `aria-label`, for example *Add*.
- A loading button stays focusable and keeps its name; it is announced as busy (`aria-busy`) and does not react to clicks.
- A disabled button is a native disabled button: it is skipped by Tab and announced as unavailable.
- Focus uses the system blue ring. Figma draws a light grey ring (`primary-soft-200`), about 1.2:1 against the page, which keyboard users can barely see.
- Colours follow Figma. Known low contrast: the pressed Secondary button in destructive (2.6:1) and green (about 3:1) colours; green text on the page is at the 4.5:1 limit.
