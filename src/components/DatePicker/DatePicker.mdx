A date field with a calendar under it, for one date or a period. People can type the date in their own format or choose it in the calendar.
For filters it adds ready-made periods (presets), two months side by side, and Reset / Apply.

## Examples

Every example is a live story. Its steps also run as a test in the **Interactions** tab, so a broken behaviour shows up at once.

### Pick a due date

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.PickADueDate} />

A click on the field opens the calendar. Choosing a day fills the field and closes the calendar; focus returns to the field.

### Type or use the keyboard

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.TypeOrUseTheKeyboard} />

Digits fall into the locale's format as people type (*05012026* → *05/01/2026* in the UK). ↓ opens the calendar on that day; the arrows move by day, PageUp and PageDown by month, Enter chooses, Escape closes and returns to the field.

### Choose a period

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.ChooseAPeriod} />

Two months and the presets for a report filter. A preset fills the period in the calendar and moves it into view. Nothing changes until **Apply**, so the data behind the filter isn't reloaded on every click.

### Follow the locale

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.FollowTheLocale} />

The same date in three locales. Open each one: the week starts on Monday in the UK and Ukraine, on Sunday in the US; month and day names follow the language.

### Jump to another year

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.JumpToAnotherYear} />

The month and year in the header are lists (the system's [Dropdown Menu](?path=/docs/components-dropdown-menu--docs)), for dates far from today.

### Limit the dates

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.LimitTheDates} />

`minDate`, `maxDate` and `disabledDates` turn days grey; they can't be chosen and the arrow keys skip them.

### Choose a month

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.ChooseAMonth} />

### Start over

## Design decisions

What the code adds to the Figma file, and why:

- **The format follows the locale.** Figma's examples show both *16/01/2016* and *MM/DD/YYYY*. In code nothing is fixed: the order of day, month and year, the separator, the first day of the week and the month and day names all come from the locale the product passes (`locale`), else from the page language, else from the browser. The field and the calendar read the same description, so they always agree. The **Locale** switch in the toolbar shows it on every page.
- **Two months move together.** Figma draws arrows and lists in both months. In code, as in PrimeReact, the months always follow each other: the arrows sit on the outer edges and the lists in the first month.
- **Our field, PrimeReact's calendar.** The field is the system's Input (label, hint, error, the same frame as other fields); the calendar is PrimeReact Calendar inside a panel under it.
- **Apply for filters.** PrimeReact applies a date at once and offers Today / Clear. Figma's Reset / Apply apply only on Apply, which suits filters; they are on by default for a period and off for one date (`buttons`).
- **Today in words.** Figma marks today with a gold dot; screen readers hear the full date and "today".
- **Days named in full.** Each day is a grid cell named *Monday, 5 January 2026*, not just *5*.
- **"Mon", not "Mo".** The Figma *Day label* part shows *Mo*, the calendar *Mon*; the code uses the short names of the locale, as the calendar does.
- **Mobile later.** The Figma bottom sheet (*Specify a period*) waits for the Modal component.

## Types

<Canvas of={DatePickerStories.Types} />

The open calendar on its own (`DatePickerPanel`, the Figma *Date picker*): **Default** for one date, **Range** with two months and Reset / Apply, and **Presets** for filters.
The panel has 24px padding (16px on the left with presets), 16px corners and the small elevation; two months sit 24px apart.

## Parts

Figma ships the calendar's pieces as their own components. In code they are parts of Date Picker:

- **Day label:** the weekday names, Body-md in the soft colour.
- **Day cell:** 40 × 40. Hovered: a light fill. Active (chosen): black with white text. Disabled and days of other months: grey. Current: the gold dot. In a period: a band joins the start and finish squares.
- **Month:** 88 × 40 cells of the month grid, with the same Hovered and Active look.
- **Period range:** a row of the preset list; hovered with a light fill, selected with a fill and a check.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Typing | Fills the date in the locale's format |
| ↓ (in the field) | Opens the calendar and moves focus to the chosen day, or today |
| ← → ↑ ↓ | Move by day and week |
| PageUp · PageDown | Previous and next month |
| Home · End | First and last day of the week |
| Enter | Chooses the day |
| Escape | Closes the calendar and returns to the field |
| Tab | Moves to the presets, the lists, the arrows and the buttons |

- The field is a combobox that opens a dialog (`aria-haspopup="dialog"`, `aria-expanded`); the calendar is a named dialog with a grid of days.
- The chosen day is announced as selected; disabled days are announced as unavailable.
- The presets are a listbox; ↑ ↓ move and the chosen period is announced as selected.
- Colours follow Figma. Days of other months and disabled days use `text-disabled-400`, below 4.5:1, as disabled controls may.
