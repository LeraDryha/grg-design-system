Switches between views of the same place: All and Unread, the parts of a form, sections of a settings page. Horizontal with a gold underline, or vertical with a gold bar.

## Design decisions

- **PrimeReact TabView** gives the tab and panel roles and the keys; the tabs are the Figma items. PrimeReact moves with ← / → only, so vertical tabs also move with ↑ / ↓.
- **The open tab** shows the strongest text and icon and the gold line (horizontal, under it) or bar (vertical, on the left); hover takes the strong text only.
- **Focus** uses the system blue ring.

## Layouts

<Canvas of={TabStories.Layouts} />

- **Horizontal**: 24px apart, a line under the row. Icons only: 16px apart.
- **Vertical**: 40px tabs, 2px apart, with an optional title, badge, arrow or Transaction Icon.

## Accessibility

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Tab | Moves to the open tab, then into its content |
| ← / → (↑ / ↓ vertical) | Moves between tabs |
| Home / End | First and last tab |
| Enter / Space | Opens the tab with focus |

- A `tablist` named by its title or `aria-label`, tabs with `aria-selected`, and the content as a `tabpanel`.
- Known contrast from Figma: a disabled tab (`text-disabled-400`) is under 4.5:1.
