The top of every page: its title, an arrow back when the page was opened from a list, and the page's buttons at the end. It came out of the In product audit: each page built its own top, and moving between pages the title and the content jumped by a few pixels.

## Design decisions

- **Designed in code** from the In product pages (after the audit): Title-lg, buttons sm, 12px between them; the content starts 20px under it, as in every Figma frame.
- **The main button last**, at the right edge; a second one, outline, before it.
- **Phones**: the buttons go under the title, in a row that wraps.
- **No PrimeReact**: its Toolbar would be announced as a toolbar and moved through with the arrow keys; this is a heading and buttons, reached with Tab.

## Accessibility

- The title is the page's only `h1`.
- The back arrow is a button with a name; Tab reaches it first, then the buttons at the end.
