# Coin icons

<ComponentHeader status="ready" figma="80-4965" guidelines="80-14858" />

Coin icons represent cryptocurrencies, tokens and digital assets, plus four fiat currencies (USD, EUR, CNY, UAH).
The set has 682 icons on a 24 × 24 grid, in each coin's own colours, exported from Figma under their Figma names.

## Sizes

<PictureSizes set="coin" icon="bitcoin-btc" sizes={[{ size: 24, label: '24 × 24' }, { size: 20, label: '20 × 20' }]} />

Coin icons are used in two sizes: 24 × 24 and 20 × 20. Use the size that matches the component and its text,
so the interface stays consistent. The Figma page calls these three sizes and then lists two; the two listed are available here.

## Using coin icons

- Show the coin's name or ticker next to the icon: many logos look alike, and people check the ticker before they trade.
- Don't recolour coin icons: the colours are part of the logo. They stay the same in both themes, except for coins on a black disc
  (XRP, EOS, Algorand and 43 more): on the dark theme their black and white swap, so they don't disappear. Figma has no dark set; this is the code's addition.
- Icons are square and scale without distortion, like an icon with a locked aspect ratio in Figma.
- Icons are decorative when the name is written next to them: screen readers skip them. Use `label` only for an icon
  that stands alone, such as a coin in a compact table cell.

```tsx
<CoinIcon name="bitcoin-btc" size={20} />
```

The key is the Figma name in lower case, with anything other than letters and digits turned into hyphens:
*Bitcoin (BTC)* → `bitcoin-btc`, *USD Coin (USDC)* → `usd-coin-usdc`. Search the list below by name or ticker.
