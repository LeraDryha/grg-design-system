# Social icons

<ComponentHeader status="ready" figma="68-17086" guidelines="68-17669" />

Social icons represent social media platforms and external social links: sign in with Google or Apple, share, follow us.
The set has 25 platforms on a 24 × 24 grid, exported from Figma under their Figma names, in two styles.

## Styles

- **Brand:** the platform's own colours. Use it where people need to recognise the platform at a glance, such as *Sign in with Google*.
- **Gray:** one colour, the `icon-base-500` token. Use it where several platforms sit together and should stay quiet, such as a footer.
  Slack, Zapier, Notion and Dropbox have no Gray version in Figma and stay Brand.

Brand colours are the same in both themes. Seven marks are black in Figma (X, Apple, GitHub, TikTok, Tumblr, AngelList, Layers)
and would disappear on dark, so in the dark theme they turn white. Figma has no dark Brand set; this is the code's addition.

## Sizes

<SocialIconSizes />

Social icons are used in two sizes: 24 × 24 and 20 × 20. Use the size that matches the component and its text,
so the interface stays consistent. The Figma page calls these three sizes and then lists two; the two listed are available here.

## Using social icons

- Use social icons only for social platforms. For everything else, use the [Base icons](?path=/docs/foundations-icons-base-icons--docs).
- Set the size with `size`. Icons are square and scale without distortion, like an icon with a locked aspect ratio in Figma.
- Don't recolour Brand icons: the colours are part of the logo. For one colour, use Gray. Don't mix the two styles in one group.
- Icons are decorative: screen readers skip them. When an icon is the only content of a link or button, give the link or button
  a name (*LinkedIn*, *Sign in with Apple*). Use `label` only for a standalone icon that means something by itself.

```tsx
<SocialIcon platform="linkedin" variant="gray" size={20} />
```

The platform is the Figma name in lower case: *Facebook* → `facebook`, *X (Twitter)* → `x`. The Figma Style is `variant`: `brand` or `gray`.
