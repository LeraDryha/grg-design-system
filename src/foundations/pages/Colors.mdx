# Colors

Colors come in two layers. **Primitives** are the raw palette (`--color-gold-500`). **Semantic tokens** say what a color is for
(`--text-strong-1000`, `--surface-base`) and point at a primitive. Components use only semantic tokens, so switching
to the dark theme swaps the primitives behind them and every component follows.
